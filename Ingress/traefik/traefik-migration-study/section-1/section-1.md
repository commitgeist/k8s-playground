Kubernetes SIGS

Kubernetes Special Interest Groups (SIGs) are community-driven, specialized teams that manage distinct areas of the Kubernetes open-source project. They are the core operational structure of the project, allowing a large and complex system to be developed and maintained by a diverse, decentralized community.
